# Portfolio Case Study: Marketplace Opportunity Discovery Dashboard

## Problem
Marketplace teams have many potential seller-growth opportunities but limited capacity. They need a consistent way to identify high-potential segments, quantify execution gaps, and turn analysis into a prioritized product roadmap.

## Solution
I built a working analytics MVP that ingests marketplace seller data, scores opportunities using transparent business signals, visualizes growth potential, and recommends the next product initiative.

## PM Method
- Size opportunity using GMV and growth momentum.
- Identify gaps in conversion, listing quality, advertising efficiency, inventory, and returns.
- Combine signals into a transparent opportunity score.
- Turn the highest-ranked opportunity into a product initiative and measurable success metric.

## Example Insight
A high-growth seller segment with healthy inventory and strong demand, but weak listing quality or conversion, is a strong candidate for an AI listing-quality and conversion playbook before additional acquisition investment.

## Resume Bullet
Built a marketplace opportunity discovery dashboard that scored seller-growth opportunities using GMV momentum, conversion, listing quality, advertising efficiency, inventory readiness, and return rate; translated the analysis into a prioritized AI product initiative and success metrics.
