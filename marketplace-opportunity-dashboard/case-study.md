# Portfolio Case Study: Marketplace Opportunity Discovery Dashboard

## Problem
Marketplace teams have many potential seller-growth opportunities but limited capacity. They need a consistent way to identify high-potential segments, quantify execution gaps, and turn analysis into a prioritized product roadmap.

## Solution
I built a working analytics MVP that ingests marketplace seller data, scores opportunities using transparent business signals, visualizes growth potential, and recommends the next product initiative.

## PM Method
- Size opportunity using GMV and growth momentum.
- Identify gaps in conversion, listing quality, advertising efficiency, inventory, and returns.
- Combine signals into a transparent opportunity score.
- Turn the highest-ranked opportunity into a product initiative and measurable success metric.

## Example Insight
A high-growth seller segment with healthy inventory and strong demand, but weak listing quality or conversion, is a strong candidate for an AI listing-quality and conversion playbook before additional acquisition investment.

## Stakeholder Collaboration Plan

| Stakeholder | Need or concern | PM interaction | Shared decision / metric |
|---|---|---|---|
| Category and country teams | Local market opportunity and practical actions | Weekly insight review | Segment selection and GMV opportunity |
| Seller Success | Which sellers need intervention first | Backlog calibration workshop | Seller coverage and action completion |
| Data Science / Analytics | Valid metrics and scoring methodology | Weight-review and historical back-testing | Score precision and data quality |
| Engineering | Feasible data pipeline and dashboard scope | Technical scoping and delivery planning | Data freshness and reliability |
| Finance | Credible value sizing | Assumption review | Incremental GMV and cost-to-serve |
| Leadership | Investment priorities | Roadmap decision meeting | Return on investment and strategic fit |

## Cross-functional Decision Story

Different teams may each advocate for their own category or country. The PM uses a shared scoring framework so prioritization is repeatable rather than driven only by stakeholder volume. Finance validates the opportunity-sizing assumptions, analytics reviews score weights, and seller-success teams validate whether an intervention is operationally actionable. The output is a transparent ranked backlog that leadership can challenge, revise, and approve.

## Launch Plan

1. Align metric definitions with analytics and finance.
2. Review initial opportunity backlog with category and country leaders.
3. Validate top opportunities with seller-success teams before committing roadmap capacity.
4. Pilot one selected initiative; compare business impact with an equivalent non-pilot segment.
5. Recalibrate score weights using observed intervention results.
