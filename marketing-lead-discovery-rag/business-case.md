# Estimated Business Case: Marketing Lead Discovery RAG

## Scope of This Estimate

This is a planning model, not a claim of realized value. Replace every assumption with measured pilot data before presenting benefits as validated.

## Pilot Measures Before ROI

- Research time per accepted profile
- Profile acceptance, edit, and rejection rate
- Source freshness and citation correctness
- Researcher repeat use
- Cost per accepted profile
- Downstream outreach readiness

Incremental revenue should not be claimed until a controlled design can attribute an outcome to the lead-discovery workflow.
