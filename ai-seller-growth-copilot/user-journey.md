# Seller Lifecycle and User Journey

## Seller Lifecycle

Onboarding → Listing Creation → Traffic Acquisition → Conversion Optimization → Advertising Optimization → Retention and Growth

## Pain Points by Stage

| Stage | Seller Goal | Pain Point | AI Opportunity |
|---|---|---|---|
| Onboarding | Launch first products | Does not know marketplace requirements | Guided setup checklist |
| Listing Creation | Create high-quality listings | Weak titles, images, keywords | Listing quality assistant |
| Traffic Acquisition | Get product views | Low discoverability | Keyword and category suggestions |
| Conversion Optimization | Turn views into orders | Poor content, price, reviews | Conversion diagnosis |
| Advertising Optimization | Improve ROAS | Budget waste, weak keywords | Ad performance recommendations |
| Retention and Growth | Scale business | Hard to prioritize actions | Weekly AI action plan |

## Core User Journey
1. Seller sees growth alert: Sales dropped 18% week over week.
2. Seller opens AI Seller Growth Copilot.
3. Copilot identifies that sessions are stable but conversion declined.
4. Copilot compares listing quality, price, reviews, and inventory.
5. Copilot recommends three actions.
6. Seller accepts two actions.
7. Copilot tracks conversion rate and sales change over 14 days.
