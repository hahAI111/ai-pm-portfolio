# Seller Case Example: Conversion Drop Diagnosis

## Seller Profile
- Seller type: Growth-stage cross-border seller
- Category: Home organization products
- Marketplace maturity: 14 months selling history
- Business goal: Increase sales while keeping advertising spend efficient

## Business Context
The seller noticed that weekly sales dropped by 18%, but advertising spend stayed roughly the same. The seller was unsure whether the problem came from traffic, listing quality, pricing, reviews, or ad efficiency.

## Input Signals

| Signal | Recent Change | Interpretation |
|---|---:|---|
| Sessions | +2% | Traffic is stable |
| Conversion rate | -21% | Main issue is likely conversion |
| Buy Box / price competitiveness | Slightly weaker | Price may be less attractive |
| Listing quality score | Medium | Listing has improvement room |
| Review rating | Stable | Reviews are unlikely the main cause |
| Inventory | Healthy | No stockout issue |
| Ad ROAS | -16% | Ads are driving traffic but not converting |

## AI Diagnosis
The Copilot determines that the sales decline is most likely caused by a conversion issue rather than a traffic issue. The evidence is that sessions remained stable while conversion rate dropped significantly. Secondary contributing factors may include weaker price competitiveness and medium listing quality.

## Recommended Action Plan

| Priority | Action | Reason | Expected Impact | Effort | Metric to Watch |
|---|---|---|---|---|---|
| P0 | Improve title and first two bullet points | Listing content may not communicate value clearly | Medium to high | Low | Conversion rate |
| P1 | Test a 7-day promotional price | Price competitiveness weakened | Medium | Medium | Conversion rate and margin |
| P1 | Replace weak secondary product images | Product benefits may not be clear visually | Medium | Medium | Add-to-cart rate |
| P2 | Pause low-converting ad keywords | ROAS declined while traffic stayed stable | Medium | Low | ROAS |

## Seller-facing AI Response Example
Your sales decline appears to be driven by conversion, not traffic. Sessions increased by 2%, but conversion rate dropped by 21%. Inventory and reviews look stable, so the most likely causes are listing quality and price competitiveness. I recommend starting with listing improvements because they are lower effort and can improve both organic and paid traffic conversion.

## PM Notes
This case shows why the product should not only generate advice, but also explain the evidence behind the recommendation. Seller trust depends on transparency, prioritization, and measurable follow-up.
