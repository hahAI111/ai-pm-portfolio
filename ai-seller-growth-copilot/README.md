# AI Seller Growth Copilot

## One-line Summary
An AI-powered product concept that helps cross-border e-commerce sellers diagnose growth blockers, improve listings, optimize advertising decisions, and generate action plans across the seller lifecycle.

## Target Role Alignment
This project is designed for AI Product Manager roles focused on seller growth, marketplace products, AI/ML implementation, roadmap ownership, and operational efficiency.

## Core Deliverables
- Product Requirements Document (PRD)
- Seller lifecycle and user journey map
- MVP feature scope
- AI solution design
- Metrics and experiment plan
- Resume bullets and interview story

## Recommended Project Narrative
I identified common growth blockers across the global seller lifecycle and designed an AI copilot that combines seller performance signals, marketplace knowledge, and LLM-generated recommendations to help sellers take faster, higher-quality growth actions.
