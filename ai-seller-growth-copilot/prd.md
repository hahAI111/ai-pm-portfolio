# PRD: AI Seller Growth Copilot

## 1. Product Vision
Help cross-border marketplace sellers grow faster by turning fragmented performance data, marketplace guidance, and operational best practices into clear AI-generated recommendations and prioritized action plans.

## 2. Problem Statement
Cross-border sellers often struggle to understand why growth is slowing. Their data is spread across listing quality, traffic, conversion, advertising, inventory, and policy surfaces. Many sellers know there is a problem, but do not know which action to take first.

## 3. Target Users
- Growth-stage seller: has active products and wants to improve traffic, conversion, and advertising efficiency.
- New seller: needs guidance on listing quality, pricing, and first sales.
- Account manager: needs scalable diagnosis and consistent recommendations.

## 4. MVP Scope
- Growth Diagnosis: identify low traffic, low conversion, weak listing quality, inefficient ads, inventory issues, and pricing issues.
- Listing Optimization Assistant: improve title, bullets, description, keywords, and image checklist.
- Action Plan Generator: rank weekly actions by expected impact and effort.
- Seller Q&A: answer seller questions using marketplace knowledge.

## 5. Key User Flow
1. Seller opens Growth Copilot dashboard.
2. System summarizes current performance.
3. Seller sees top growth blockers.
4. AI explains likely causes and evidence.
5. AI recommends prioritized actions.
6. Seller accepts, edits, or dismisses actions.
7. System tracks outcome metrics over time.

## 6. Roadmap
- Phase 1: MVP diagnosis and recommendations.
- Phase 2: Personalized seller playbooks.
- Phase 3: Experimentation loop.
- Phase 4: Workflow integration.

## 7. Risks and Mitigations
| Risk | Mitigation |
|---|---|
| Generic advice | Ground responses in seller data and marketplace knowledge |
| Incorrect recommendation | Add confidence levels and human review for high-impact actions |
| Low seller trust | Explain evidence and show source data |
| Hard to prove impact | Track accepted actions and downstream metric movement |
