## Short Interview Pitch
I built a product case for an AI Seller Growth Copilot aimed at helping cross-border marketplace sellers identify growth blockers and take prioritized actions. I started from the seller lifecycle, mapped pain points across onboarding, listing creation, traffic, conversion, advertising, and growth, then designed an MVP that combines rule-based diagnosis, retrieval-augmented guidance, and LLM-generated action plans. I also defined the roadmap, success metrics, guardrails, and A/B test plan to evaluate seller adoption and business impact.

## STAR Story

### Situation
Cross-border sellers often face fragmented data and unclear guidance when sales decline or growth slows.

### Task
Design an AI product that helps sellers diagnose growth issues and take measurable actions while supporting marketplace business growth.

### Action
I mapped the seller lifecycle, identified high-frequency pain points, defined MVP features, designed the AI solution architecture, and created a metrics framework. The product used structured seller data for diagnosis, marketplace knowledge retrieval for grounding, and LLM-generated recommendations for seller-friendly action plans.

### Result
The concept created a clear end-to-end AI product story covering opportunity identification, product strategy, AI implementation, roadmap planning, and success measurement.
