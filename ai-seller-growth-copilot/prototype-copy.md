## Page 2: Diagnosis Detail

### Diagnosis
Your sales decline is likely caused by conversion, not traffic.

### Evidence
- Sessions increased by 2%, so product visibility is not the main issue.
- Conversion rate dropped by 21%, which explains most of the sales decline.
- Inventory and reviews are stable, so they are unlikely to be the primary cause.
- Price competitiveness weakened slightly, and listing quality has room to improve.

### Confidence
Medium-high confidence

### Why not traffic?
If traffic were the main issue, sessions would have dropped together with sales. Since sessions are stable, the issue is more likely happening after shoppers land on the product page.
